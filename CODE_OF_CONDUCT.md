# Community conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive and considerate of
other people's experience and time. Critique code and ideas, not people. Welcome
questions, explain technical disagreements and respect privacy and accessibility.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
and sharing others' private information without permission are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to repository discussions, reviews, issues and other
project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, pause discussions, warn participants or
restrict participation proportionately. Explain moderation decisions privately
where possible, consider context and allow a review by an uninvolved maintainer.
Do not retaliate against good-faith reports. Maintainers involved in a complaint
should not decide its outcome.

## Reporting and enforcement readiness

Use an established private project-maintainer contact for current restricted
collaboration. Do not post sensitive incident details in public issues.
**Before public launch, the owner must designate a private conduct contact and an
independent escalation route, and publish them here.** No monitored address or
independent moderation team is implied today. The missing contact is an explicit
[public-launch blocker](docs/community-release.md), not a completed safeguard.
